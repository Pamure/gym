## PUSH EXERCISES

### Flat Barbell Bench Press
- **Muscles:** chest (primary), front delts, triceps
- **Form cues:**
  1. Shoulder blades pinched together and DOWN before unracking
  2. Feet flat, slight upper-back arch, butt stays on bench
  3. Lower bar to nipple line, elbows ~45–70° from torso (not flared 90°)
  4. Press up and slightly back toward your face
- **Mistakes:** bouncing bar off chest; hips lifting; half reps; grip too wide
- **GIF:** `bench-press` · **Video:** `rT7DgCr-3pg`

### Standing Overhead Press (OHP)
- **Muscles:** shoulders (primary), triceps, upper chest, core
- **Form cues:**
  1. Squeeze glutes and brace abs BEFORE pressing (kills back-lean)
  2. Bar starts on front delts; move head back, press straight up, head through
  3. Lock out over shoulders, biceps by ears
- **Mistakes:** leaning back into a standing incline press; flaring ribs; no lockout
- **GIF:** `overhead-press` · **Video:** `_RlRDWO2jfg`

### Incline Dumbbell Press
- **Muscles:** upper chest, front delts, triceps
- **Form cues:**
  1. Bench 30–45° (steeper = more shoulder, less chest)
  2. Dumbbells travel up and slightly inward; full stretch at bottom
  3. Control 2–3 s down
- **Mistakes:** bench too steep; bouncing dumbbells at top; shallow range
- **GIF:** `incline-dumbbell-press` · **Video:** `8iPEnn-ltC8`

### Tricep Rope Pushdown
- **Muscles:** triceps
- **Form cues:**
  1. Elbows PINNED to sides — only forearms move
  2. Push down, split rope apart at the bottom
  3. Squeeze 1 s at full extension
- **Mistakes:** body swinging; elbows drifting forward; too heavy
- **GIF:** `tricep-pushdown` · **Video:** `2-LAMcpzODU`

### Dumbbell Lateral Raise
- **Muscles:** side delts (shoulder width — key for the aesthetic V-taper)
- **Form cues:**
  1. Slight forward lean, soft elbows
  2. Lead with the ELBOW, raise to shoulder height only
  3. Slow 2–3 s lowering — half the growth is in the negative
- **Mistakes:** momentum swinging; shrugging traps; too heavy (this exercise punishes ego hardest)
- **GIF:** `lateral-raise` · **Video:** `OuG1smZTsQQ`

### Dumbbell Bench Press
- **Muscles:** chest, front delts, triceps
- **Form cues:** same as barbell bench + deeper stretch at bottom, dumbbells over mid-chest
- **Mistakes:** dumbbells drifting apart; clanking at top to rest
- **GIF:** `dumbbell-bench-press` · **Video:** `8iPEnn-ltC8`

### Dumbbell Overhead Press
- **Muscles:** shoulders, triceps
- **Form cues:** same bracing as barbell OHP; press from chin level to lockout
- **Mistakes:** lower-back arch; pressing forward instead of up
- **GIF:** `dumbbell-shoulder-press` · **Video:** `_RlRDWO2jfg`

## LEG EXERCISES

### Barbell Back Squat
- **Muscles:** quads (primary), glutes, core, entire lower body
- **Form cues (Nippard 5-point checklist):**
  1. Bar on upper traps (high-bar), grip tight, elbows down
  2. Walk out in 3 steps max
  3. Stance just outside shoulder width, toes slightly out
  4. Big belly breath, brace, sit DOWN and slightly back
  5. Break parallel (hip crease below knee), drive up, knees track over toes
- **Mistakes:** knees caving in; lower-back rounding at the bottom ("butt wink" — reduce weight,
  work ankle mobility); heels lifting; no brace
- **GIF:** `back-squat` · **Video:** `gcNh17Ckjgg`

### Goblet Squat (Month 1 squat teacher)
- **Muscles:** quads, glutes, core
- **Form cues:** dumbbell held vertically at chest; elbows track inside knees; sit deep, chest proud
- **Why first:** teaches depth + upright torso with near-zero spinal risk
- **GIF:** `goblet-squat` · **Video:** `gcNh17Ckjgg`

### Front Squat (Month 2–3, Friday)
- **Muscles:** quads (more than back squat), heavy core demand
- **Form cues:** bar on front delts, elbows HIGH (parallel to floor), torso upright
- **Mistakes:** elbows dropping (bar rolls forward); leaning forward
- **GIF:** `front-squat` · **Video:** search fallback `Jeff Nippard front squat technique`

### Romanian Deadlift (RDL)
- **Muscles:** hamstrings (primary), glutes, lower back
- **Form cues:**
  1. Soft knees — then knees LOCKED in that position for the whole set
  2. Push hips BACK — it's a hinge, not a squat
  3. Bar slides down thighs, stays touching legs
  4. Stop at deep hamstring stretch; squeeze glutes to stand
- **Mistakes:** rounding back; bending knees more on the way down (becomes a squat); chasing the
  floor when hamstrings say stop — range is set by YOUR mobility, not the floor
- **GIF:** `romanian-deadlift` · **Video:** `_oyxCn2iSjU`

### Leg Press
- **Muscles:** quads, glutes
- **Form cues:** feet shoulder-width mid-platform; lower to 90° knees; push through heels;
  never lock knees at top
- **Mistakes:** butt lifting off pad at bottom (lower-back risk — shorten range); quarter reps
  with the whole stack (classic ego lift)
- **GIF:** `leg-press` · **Video:** `IZxyjW7MPJQ`

### Lying Leg Curl
- **Muscles:** hamstrings
- **Form cues:** pad just above ankles; hips glued to bench; full curl, slow negative
- **Mistakes:** hips rising; momentum
- **GIF:** `lying-leg-curl` · **Video:** `1Tq3QdYUuHs`

### Standing Calf Raise
- **Muscles:** calves (gastrocnemius + soleus)
- **Form cues:** FULL stretch at bottom + 1 s pause; explode up; 1 s squeeze at top; no bouncing
- **Mistakes:** bouncing off the Achilles tendon; half range; rushing
- **GIF:** `standing-calf-raise` · **Video:** `JbyjNymZOt0`
