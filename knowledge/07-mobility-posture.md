# 07 — Warm-up, mobility and posture

## Warm-up before lifting (5–8 minutes)

A warm-up is rehearsal, not a test:

1. **Raise:** easy treadmill, bike or walk for 2–3 minutes.
2. **Activate:** a few bodyweight squats, glute bridges or light band pulls.
3. **Mobilize:** comfortable arm circles, hip circles, leg swings or cat-cow.
4. **Potentiate:** one or two light practice sets of the first exercise.

Long static stretching is optional and is usually more useful after training or
on a recovery day. It is not a magic injury shield; load, technique, recovery
and individual history matter too.

## Mobility and posture

No single drill permanently “fixes” posture. Rows, pulldowns, face pulls,
planks and dead bugs give a beginner useful strength and awareness. Gentle
chest, hip-flexor and thoracic-rotation stretches may feel good after sitting.
Use a pain-free range and breathe normally.

The app's optional recovery circuit can include doorway chest stretch, kneeling
hip-flexor stretch, cat-cow, thread-the-needle and a comfortable walk. Stop if
there is sharp pain, numbness, dizziness or worsening symptoms.

## Cool-down

Walk until breathing settles. If stretching feels good, hold a mild stretch for
20–30 seconds without bouncing or forcing a joint. A cool-down is a transition,
not a requirement for preventing soreness.
