# 07 — Mobility, Warm-up & Posture Correction

## 1. The RAMP Warm-up (before EVERY gym session, 5–8 min, in the app as a checklist)

Science: dynamic warm-ups raise tissue temperature and prepare the nervous system; long static
stretching BEFORE lifting temporarily reduces strength — keep static stretches for after.

| Phase | What | Examples | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R**aise | Heart rate up, blood flowing | Brisk treadmill walk, jumping jacks | 2 min |
| **A**ctivate | Wake target muscles | Glute bridges, bodyweight squats, band pull-aparts | 2 min |
| **M**obilize | Joints through range | Arm circles, hip circles, leg swings, cat-cow | 2 min |
| **P**otentiate | Rehearse the first lift | 2 warm-up sets: 50% × 5, then 70% × 3 | 2 min |

Never skip this. Cold muscles + heavy load = the #1 beginner injury mechanism.

## 2. Posture Correction Plan (why you won't "look weird")

Your concern is valid and common: desk/phone posture + untrained back = rounded shoulders,
forward head. The program attacks it three ways:

1. **Strengthen the weak:** upper back (rows Wed + Fri), rear delts/rotator cuff (face pulls
   every Wednesday — never skip them), deep core (Thursday). The program keeps push:pull volume
   at 1:1 deliberately; mirror-muscle-only training is what creates the hunched gym-bro look.
2. **Stretch the tight (Thursday mobility circuit):**
   - Doorway chest stretch — 30 s/side (tight pecs anchor shoulders forward)
   - Hip flexor lunge stretch — 30 s/side (sitting shortens these → anterior pelvic tilt)
   - Cat-cow — 10 reps (spinal mobility)
   - Thread-the-needle — 8/side (thoracic rotation)
   - World's greatest stretch — 5/side (full-body opener)
   - Neck side tilts — 20 s/side ("tech neck" release)
3. **Daily habit:** the thumbs test — stand relaxed, look down: if your thumbs point toward each
   other/thighs, shoulders are internally rotated → roll them back, chest up, 10×/day.

## 3. Post-Workout Cool-down (3–5 min, after every session)
- Easy walk until breathing normalizes
- Static stretches for the muscles trained that day, 30 s each, mild tension (never pain)

## 4. Saturday Active Recovery (full routine)
1. 20 min light cardio — conversational pace (this builds your stamina base)
2. Foam roll 1 min each: quads, hamstrings, glutes, upper back, lats
3. Static stretch circuit 30 s each: hamstrings, hip flexors, chest, shoulders, calves
4. Optional kegel set

## 5. Flexibility expectations
- Week 1–4: hamstrings/hip flexors loosen noticeably; squat depth improves
- Week 5–8: shoulders sit further back naturally; deeper, easier stretches
- Week 9–12: posture change visible in photos; take the same-pose photo monthly to see it

**Rule:** stretching = mild tension, breathe, hold. Never bounce, never push into pain.
