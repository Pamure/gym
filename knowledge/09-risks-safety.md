# 09 — Risks, Failure Modes & Safety Rules

Fact: ~55% of exercise injuries occur in the first 3 months. Nearly all are preventable.
This program's defenses, and what to watch for:

## The Big 5 Beginner Killers

1. **Ego lifting** — loading weight your form can't handle to not "look weak."
   Signs: half-reps, swinging/bouncing, back rounding, irritation when corrected.
   Antidote: nobody in the gym is watching you; everyone was a beginner; the 68 kg guy lifting
   40 kg perfectly gets respect, the guy quarter-squatting 140 kg gets memes. The app enforces
   RIR targets — respect them.
2. **Too much, too soon** — tendons/ligaments adapt slower than muscles.
   Antidote: ≤10% load increase per week; double-progression method (knowledge/01).
3. **Skipping the warm-up** — cold tissue + load = strains.
   Antidote: RAMP checklist is the first screen of every workout in the app. Non-negotiable.
4. **Skipping recovery** — muscle grows during rest, not training.
   Antidote: Saturday = light only, Sunday = full rest, 7–9 h sleep. More days ≠ faster results.
5. **Bad form repeated for weeks** — one sloppy rep is fine; 500 sloppy reps is an injury.
   Antidote: the Layer-3 video checks (knowledge/04). Film weekly.

## Overtraining warning signs (check monthly)
- Morning resting heart rate consistently +5–10 bpm above normal
- Persistent soreness that never resolves
- Insomnia despite exhaustion ("wired but tired")
- Motivation crash, dread of the gym
- Strength going DOWN across 2+ weeks
→ Response: deload week (same exercises, half the sets, 60% weight). If symptoms persist: full rest week.

## Pain Protocol (memorize)
- **Muscle burn during set** → normal, continue.
- **Dull muscle soreness 24–72 h later (DOMS)** → normal, worst in weeks 1–3, fades with adaptation.
- **Sharp/stabbing/pinching pain, especially in joints or lower back** → STOP that exercise
  immediately. Note it in the app. Do not "push through." Substitute the machine version of the
  movement for 2 weeks, or drop it entirely.
- **Pain persisting >3 days, or any radiating pain/numbness** → doctor/physio. Not optional.

## Exercise-specific danger points
- **Deadlift/squat:** lower-back rounding under load = end the set. Never "grind" a rounded rep in months 1–2.
- **Bench press alone:** if you might fail, don't clip the collars... actually for a beginner: use
  the SAFETY ARMS on the rack, or use dumbbells when training alone. Never bench heavy alone without safeties.
- **OHP:** if lower back arches hard, the weight is too heavy — sit down and use dumbbells.
- **Behind-the-neck anything (pulldown/press):** never. Shoulder risk, zero benefit.

## Delhi-specific practicalities
- Hydrate before you feel thirsty (3–4 L/day); summer training without water = lightheadedness on compounds.
- If the gym is crowded, swap exercise ORDER freely (do the free exercise first), never swap the warm-up.
- Air quality bad day? Keep Saturday cardio indoors.

## The one-line safety summary
**Perfect reps with boring weights for 12 weeks beats heroic weights for 2 weeks — every time.**
