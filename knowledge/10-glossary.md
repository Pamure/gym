# 10 — Gym Glossary (Every Term in Plain Language)

The app's Learn tab renders this. Written for someone on day one.

## The basics
- **Rep (repetition):** one complete movement — one push-up = 1 rep.
- **Set:** a group of reps done back-to-back, then rest. "3×10" = 3 sets of 10 reps with rest between.
- **Rest period:** the break between sets. Compounds 2–3 min, isolations 60–90 s.
- **Weight / load:** how heavy. Always log the TOTAL (a 20 kg bar + two 10 kg plates = 40 kg).
- **Rep range:** the target span, e.g. "8–12" means pick a weight where you fail to keep good
  form somewhere between rep 8 and 12.

## Intensity terms
- **RIR (Reps In Reserve):** how many more GOOD reps you could have done when you stopped.
  3 RIR = stopped with 3 left. Your program is built on this — Month 1: 3 RIR, Month 2: 2, Month 3: 1.
- **RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion):** the same idea on a 1–10 scale. RPE 7 ≈ 3 RIR.
  You only need RIR; RPE is just another dial for the same thing.
- **Failure:** the rep where form breaks or the weight won't move no matter what. Avoid in
  months 1–2 on big lifts; allowed on the last set of small exercises in month 3.
- **1RM (one-rep max):** the most you can lift once. Beginners never test it — everything here is calculated off rep ranges instead.

## Program terms
- **Progressive overload:** doing slightly more over time (more weight, more reps, better form).
  THE mechanism of all progress. Your double-progression method is the beginner-friendly version.
- **Double progression:** fill the rep range first, then add weight and start at the bottom again. (knowledge/01)
- **Compound exercise:** multiple joints/muscles at once — squat, bench, deadlift, row, OHP.
  The foundation. Most of your program.
- **Isolation exercise:** one joint, one muscle — curls, pushdowns, lateral raises, leg curls.
  The accessories.
- **Hypertrophy:** muscle growth. Reps in the 6–15 range with effort = hypertrophy training.
- **Mesocycle:** a 4-week training block. Your program = 3 mesocycles (Foundation, Development, Push).
- **Deload:** a planned easy week (half sets, lighter weight) so your body absorbs the training. Not weakness — strategy.
- **Split:** how the week divides muscles. Yours: Upper Push / Lower / Pull / Core / Full Body.
- **Superset:** two exercises back-to-back without rest. Not in months 1–2 — rest properly first.
- **Drop set:** after failure, immediately reduce weight and continue. Month-3+ technique only.
- **Volume:** total hard sets per muscle per week. Beginners grow on 5–10; more is not better yet.

## Body terms
- **DOMS (Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness):** the 24–72 h dull ache after new training. Normal, not
  an injury, fades as you adapt. Not a measure of workout quality.
- **Mind-muscle connection:** consciously feeling the target muscle work. Develops over weeks;
  the app's "which muscle burned?" check trains it.
- **Time under tension (TUT):** how long a set lasts. Controlled 2–3 s negatives make light
  weights work harder — why tempo matters more than the number on the dumbbell.
- **Posterior chain:** everything on the back side — glutes, hamstrings, back. Posture lives here.
- **Core:** not just abs — the deep muscles wrapping your spine. Trained by planks/dead bugs AND
  by bracing in every compound lift.
- **Anterior pelvic tilt:** pelvis tipped forward (arched lower back, belly out) — caused by tight
  hip flexors + weak core. Your Thursday session specifically fixes this.

## Technique terms
- **Bracing:** tightening your midsection 360° (like bracing for a punch) to protect the spine under load.
- **Valsalva maneuver:** hold the brace breath through the hard part of the rep, exhale at/after lockout.
- **Lockout:** the fully extended top of a lift (arms straight on bench/OHP, standing tall on deadlift).
- **Range of motion (ROM):** how far the joint travels. Full ROM > partial ROM with more weight. Always.
- **Negative / eccentric:** the lowering half of a rep. Control it for 2–3 s — half the growth stimulus lives there.
- **Tempo:** the speed pattern of a rep. Default here: ~2–3 s down, controlled up.
- **Bar path:** the line the bar travels. Roughly vertical on squat/bench/OHP. Big deviations = form leak.

## People-slang so nothing confuses you
- **"Working set":** the real sets at your target weight (not warm-ups).
- **"Newbie gains":** the fast progress of months 1–6. You're in it. Don't waste it.
- **"Plateau":** progress stalls for 2+ weeks → check sleep/protein/attendance first (knowledge/08).
- **"Cut/bulk":** eating to lose fat / gain muscle. You're doing neither hard — you're recomping (knowledge/05).
