# 05 — Food basics (Indian, budget-aware)

This is general education for a beginner, not a personalized diet. The app does
not know the user's age, height, medical conditions, allergies, household food,
or whether weight change is a goal. A dietitian/doctor should guide medical
conditions, eating disorders, severe weight change or food restrictions.

## Simple starting rules

- Build meals around foods you can actually afford and repeat: eggs, chicken,
  fish, tofu/soy, paneer, curd, dal, rice/roti, vegetables and fruit.
- Include a protein source regularly rather than trying to “fix” a day with one
  huge dinner. A useful sports-nutrition range often discussed for resistance
  training is roughly 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day, but it is not a requirement for day one
  and should be personalized.
- Do not force a calorie surplus or crash diet without a goal and baseline
  information. Change portions slowly and watch energy, training and body trend.
- Drink regularly, especially in Delhi heat. Water needs vary with size, food,
  weather and sweat; excessive water can also be harmful.
- Aim for a consistent sleep opportunity, commonly 7–9 hours for adults.

## Cheap meal pattern

Choose one from each group when practical:

| Protein | Carbohydrate | Plants/fats |
|---|---|---|
| Eggs, soy chunks, dal, chicken, fish, tofu, curd | Roti, rice, oats, potato, banana | Seasonal vegetables, fruit, peanuts, modest oil |

Prices in `app/lib/data/diet_database.dart` are planning estimates for Delhi,
not live market quotes. We keep serving size and price next to each item so a
budget plan can be checked instead of presenting fake precision. The current
offline catalog is intentionally modest; it is not a claimed 500-food verified
database.

## Around training

Eat a familiar meal when convenient. If hungry before the gym, a banana, roti,
oats, curd or another easy-to-digest option is reasonable. There is no need to
chase a 30-minute “anabolic window”; a normal meal later is fine. Do not change
your diet and training at the same time if you cannot tell what caused a
problem.

## Supplements

No supplement is needed to begin. Whey is a convenience food, not a requirement.
Creatine may be useful for some adults but should be discussed with a clinician
when there is a kidney condition, medication, pregnancy or other concern. Avoid
fat burners, unlabelled powders, testosterone boosters and products sold with
medical promises.
