# 05 — Nutrition (Indian Non-Vegetarian, Budget-Aware, 68 kg)

## The Only 4 Numbers That Matter

| Target | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | **110–140 g/day** (1.6–2.0 g/kg) | Muscle repair/growth — the non-negotiable |
| Calories | ~2,200–2,500/day (slight surplus) | Build muscle while slowly trimming love handles (recomposition) |
| Water | 3–4 L/day | Delhi heat + training; dehydration kills performance |
| Sleep | 7–9 h | Growth hormone + recovery — more important than any supplement |

You are NOT obese, and you want muscle — so no aggressive cutting. Slight surplus + training =
muscle up, waist slowly down. Love handles leave through the kitchen + total-body training,
**not** ab exercises (spot reduction is a myth — proven repeatedly in studies).

## Budget Protein Sources (per ~100 g unless noted)

| Food | Protein | Cost note |
|---|---|---|
| Eggs | 6 g per egg | Cheapest complete protein in India |
| Soya chunks (dry) | ~52 g | Cheapest protein per rupee, period |
| Chicken breast (cooked) | ~31 g | The gold standard |
| Paneer | ~18 g | Good but calorie-dense — moderate |
| Curd/dahi | ~8–11 g | Digestion + protein |
| Dal (cooked) | ~7–9 g | Combine with rice/roti = complete amino acids |
| Fish (rohu/surmai) | ~20 g | 1–2×/week if available/affordable |

**Rule:** in Indian households dal/paneer is a side dish. For you it's the ANCHOR of the meal.
Double the portion everyone else takes.

## Sample Day (hits ~125 g protein)

- **Early morning:** water + 5 soaked almonds + 1 banana
- **Breakfast:** 3–4 egg whites + 2 whole eggs (bhurji/boiled) + 2 rotis or oats (~30 g)
- **Mid-morning:** bowl of curd + 50 g dry roasted soya chunks (~30 g)
- **Lunch:** 150 g chicken curry + 2 rotis + rice + salad (~35 g)
- **Evening (pre-workout, 60–90 min before gym):** peanut butter brown bread + banana (~10 g)
- **Dinner (post-workout):** 100 g chicken/paneer/soya + 1–2 rotis + big vegetables (~25 g)

## Timing rules (simple)
- Protein every meal: 20–35 g each, don't save it all for dinner
- Pre-workout (60–90 min before): carbs + a little protein, nothing heavy/oily
- Post-workout (within ~2 h): normal meal is fine — the "30-minute anabolic window" is mostly myth
- Don't train fasted as a beginner (lightheadedness on squats)

## Supplements
- **Needed:** none, if the food above happens.
- **Optional + safe + evidence-backed:** whey protein (convenience only — 1 scoop ≈ 24 g,
  buy only from authorized sellers, Indian market has counterfeits); creatine monohydrate
  3–5 g/day (most-studied supplement in existence, safe for healthy kidneys, ~₹500/month,
  expect +1–2 kg water weight — that's the muscle storing water, good).
- **Avoid:** mass gainers (sugar bombs), fat burners (useless or dangerous), testosterone
  boosters (scams), anything a gym trainer sells you directly.

## Love handles reality check
They shrink when: (1) you don't eat in a large surplus, (2) protein stays high, (3) training
stays consistent. Expect visible waist change around weeks 6–10. Waist measurement (app tracks
it) matters more than scale weight — you can gain muscle + lose fat simultaneously right now
and the scale barely moves.
