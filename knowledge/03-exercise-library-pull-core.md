## PULL EXERCISES

### Conventional Deadlift
- **Muscles:** entire posterior chain — back, glutes, hamstrings, traps, grip
- **Form cues (Alan Thrall 5-step setup):**
  1. Bar over mid-foot, shins ~2–3 cm from bar
  2. Hinge down, grip just outside knees
  3. Chest up — pull the "slack" out of the bar BEFORE it leaves the floor
  4. Push the FLOOR away; bar drags up your legs
  5. Lock out by squeezing glutes — don't lean back or shrug
- **Mistakes:** rounded lower back (the dangerous one — if it happens, the set is over);
  jerking the bar; hips shooting up first; hyperextending at lockout
- **Month 1 rule:** 40–60 kg only, until every rep looks identical. Film side view weekly.
- **GIF:** `deadlift` · **Video:** `r4MzxtBKyNE`

### Barbell Bent-Over Row
- **Muscles:** upper back, lats, rear delts, biceps — **the posture king**
- **Form cues:**
  1. Hinge to ~45°, brace like you're about to be punched
  2. Pull bar to lower chest/upper abs
  3. Squeeze shoulder blades 1 s at the top
- **Mistakes:** torso bouncing up each rep (too heavy); pulling to the neck; standing too upright
- **GIF:** `bent-over-row` · **Video:** `FWJR5Ve8bnQ`

### Lat Pulldown
- **Muscles:** lats (back width), biceps
- **Form cues:** slight lean back (~15°); pull to upper chest; think "elbows to back pockets";
  control the way up — don't let the stack yank you
- **Mistakes:** pulling behind the neck (shoulder risk, zero benefit); leaning back 45° (becomes
  a row); ultra-wide grip
- **GIF:** `lat-pulldown` · **Video:** `SALxEARYS2U`

### Cable Face Pull
- **Muscles:** rear delts, rotator cuff, upper back — **shoulder-health insurance, never skip**
- **Form cues:**
  1. Cable at face height, rope attachment
  2. Pull toward your forehead, thumbs point back
  3. Externally rotate at the end (knuckles finish pointing up/back)
- **Mistakes:** too heavy (you'll lean back and heave — use less); pulling to chest instead of face
- **GIF:** `face-pull` · **Video:** `rep-qVOkqgk`

### Dumbbell Bicep Curl
- **Muscles:** biceps
- **Form cues:** elbows pinned at ribs; rotate palm up (supinate) as you curl; 2–3 s negative
- **Mistakes:** swinging the body; elbows drifting forward; half reps at the bottom
- **GIF:** `bicep-curl` · **Video:** `kwG2ipFRgFo`

### Single-Arm Dumbbell Row
- **Muscles:** lats, upper back, biceps
- **Form cues:** one knee + same-side hand on bench; pull dumbbell to your HIP (not shoulder);
  squeeze shoulder blade at top
- **Mistakes:** torso rotation to cheat the weight up; short range
- **GIF:** `single-arm-row` · **Video:** `FWJR5Ve8bnQ`

## CORE

### Front Plank
- **Muscles:** deep core (transverse abdominis), shoulders, glutes
- **Form cues:** straight line head→heels; squeeze glutes; pull elbows toward toes (isometric);
  breathe steadily — never hold breath
- **Mistakes:** hips sagging (lower-back strain) or piking up; watching the clock instead of form
- **GIF:** `plank` · **Video:** `pSHjTRCQxIw`

### Dead Bug
- **Muscles:** deep core, anti-extension strength (protects spine under heavy lifts)
- **Form cues:** lower back GLUED to floor the entire time; extend opposite arm + leg slowly;
  exhale as you extend
- **Mistakes:** back arching off floor (shorten the range until it stays down); rushing
- **GIF:** `dead-bug` · **Video:** search fallback `dead bug exercise proper form`

### Bird Dog
- **Muscles:** core stability, lower back, glutes
- **Form cues:** all fours; extend opposite arm + leg until level with torso; hips stay LEVEL
  (imagine balancing a glass of water on your lower back); 2 s hold each rep
- **Mistakes:** hip rotation; back arching; speed-repping
- **GIF:** `bird-dog` · **Video:** search fallback `bird dog exercise proper form`

## CONDITIONING

### Farmer's Walk
- **Muscles:** grip/forearms, traps, core, legs — also your stamina builder
- **Form cues:** heaviest dumbbells you can hold with PERFECT posture; shoulders down and back;
  eyes forward; short controlled steps; breathe behind the brace
- **Mistakes:** leaning to one side; shoulders shrugged to ears; looking down
- **GIF:** `farmers-walk` · **Video:** `Fkzk_RqlYig`
