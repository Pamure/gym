# 04 — Form Verification: How You KNOW You're Doing It Right

The beginner's central problem: no coach. This file is your replacement coach.

---

## The 4-Layer Verification System

### Layer 1 — Before the set (30 seconds)
1. Watch the exercise GIF in the app (it's bundled, works without internet).
2. Read the 3 form cues out loud in your head. Cues are ≤6 words each — that's deliberate;
  your brain can't hold more mid-set.
3. Do 1 light "patterning" set at ~50% weight, focusing ONLY on the cues.

### Layer 2 — During the set (the 4 self-checks)
After EVERY set, answer honestly:
1. Did the **target muscle** do the work? (e.g., bench = chest burn, not front-shoulder pinch)
2. Was every rep **identical** — same tempo, same depth? (Last rep looking wildly different = weight too heavy)
3. How many **good reps were left**? (Month 1 = 3 left, Month 2 = 2, Month 3 = 1. This is RIR — see glossary)
4. Any **sharp/joint pain**? Muscle burn = normal. Sharp, stabbing, pinching joint pain = STOP, reduce weight, check form. Pain that persists next day in a JOINT (not muscle soreness) = see knowledge/09.

### Layer 3 — Film yourself (the objective coach)
Your body lies to you — what a squat *feels* like and what it *looks* like are different.
- **What to film:** your LAST working set of squat (Tue), deadlift (Wed), bench (Mon). Once a week each is enough.
- **Angle:** side view, phone at hip height, ~3 m away, whole body + bar in frame. For knee-cave
  checks on squats, one front-view set.
- **What to look for (red flags = lower weight next session):**
  - Squat: lower back rounding at the bottom, knees collapsing inward, heels lifting
  - Deadlift: ANY lower-back rounding, hips shooting up before the bar leaves the floor
  - Bench: butt lifting off the bench, bar path wildly diagonal, elbows flared to 90°
- **Optional external eye:** post to r/formcheck (free, surprisingly good) or book ONE session
  with your gym's trainer to check the big 3 lifts.

### Layer 4 — The data check (weekly, in the app)
- Weight or reps on main lifts trending **up over weeks** = it's working. Full stop.
- Flat for 2+ weeks → in order, check: (1) sleep 7–9 h? (2) protein 110–140 g? (3) skipping
  sessions? (4) RIR honest? Only then change the program.
- Sudden strength DROP + poor sleep + elevated morning heart rate = overreaching → deload (knowledge/09).

## What "right" feels like (sensation guide)
- **Muscle burn/pump** during and after sets → normal, desired.
- **DOMS** (dull muscle soreness 24–72 h later) → normal, especially weeks 1–3. Fades as you adapt.
- **Sharp joint pain, pinching, numbness, radiating pain** → NEVER normal. Stop.
- **Lightheadedness on deadlift/squat** → you're not breathing properly; learn the brace (below).

## The Brace (Valsalva, beginner-safe version)
1. Big breath into your BELLY (not chest) — 360° expansion, like filling a barrel.
2. Clamp down like bracing for a punch.
3. Hold through the hard part of the rep, exhale past it or at lockout.
4. Never hold a brace for a whole set — re-brace each rep.

## Tempo rule (default for everything)
Controlled ~2–3 s lowering, no pause-bounce, strong but not explosive lift. If you can't control
the negative, the weight is too heavy — this single rule prevents most beginner injuries.
