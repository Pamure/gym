### Kegel Protocol (Sexual Health / Core / Posture — Daily, Not Just Gym Days)
Technique (verified science):
- Sit or lie comfortably. Inhale → ribcage expands, pelvic floor gently relaxes/lengthens.
- Exhale → gently contract/lift pelvic floor (scrotum lifts inward/upward). Do NOT squeeze glutes, thighs, or abs.
- Endurance: Hold 5-10 seconds. Fully relax equal time. Repeat 10 times.
- Power Pulses: Contract at 100% for 1 second, release 1 second. Repeat 10 times.
- Daily target: 3 sets (morning, afternoon, evening) = 30 holds + 30 pulses/day.
- Progression: If easy, progress to seated → standing (gravity increases difficulty).
- Timeline: Noticeable improvement in 6-8 weeks (clinical evidence).
